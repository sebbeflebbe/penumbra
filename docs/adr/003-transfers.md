# ADR-003 — US processors residual risk

Status: accepted
Date: 2026-08-31

Even with Postgres in Frankfurt, Supabase Inc. and Cloudflare, Inc. are US companies. Chapter V residual risk remains. Mitigations: EU region, TLS, client-side encryption of card bodies, no analytics, documented TIAs.

Rejected claiming “100% EU” or Schrems-proof adequacy we do not have.
