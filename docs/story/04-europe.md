# Chapter 04 — European operations

GDPR binds if we process EU personal data. CRA and NIS2 are alignment targets, not claimed designations. The European Accessibility Act applies from 28 June 2025. EN 301 549 is the technical bar even if EAA sectoral scope likely excludes a personal notebook. We do not claim full EAA conformance.

US-parent residual risk (CLOUD Act) for Cloudflare and Supabase Inc. is written down. Client-side encryption of card bodies is the primary mitigation, not a badge.
