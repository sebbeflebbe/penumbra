# Transfer impact assessment (Chapter V)

Cloudflare, Google, GitHub, and Supabase Inc. are US-linked. SCCs appear in vendor DPAs. Supplementary measures: EU database region, TLS 1.3, client-side encryption of card bodies so processors cannot read notes, no analytics.

Residual: CLOUD Act access to metadata and, for unencrypted fields, titles and emails. We do not claim this risk is eliminated.
